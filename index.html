What is Sport?

Sport is any physical activity played with rules, where people compete as individuals or teams. Sports keep the body fit, build discipline and teamwork, and bring communities together. From neighbourhood grounds to giant stadiums, billions of people play and watch sport every year.

🏏 Cricket

A game of patience, skill and sudden drama, played between two teams of eleven.

About the Game

Cricket is played on a large grass field with a rectangular strip called the pitch in the middle. One team bats and tries to score runs. The other team bowls and fields, trying to get the batters out. The teams then swap roles, and the side with more runs wins.

Key Terms

Over

A set of 6 legal balls bowled by one bowler.

Wicket

A batter is out when dismissed, for example bowled, caught or LBW.

Boundary

A shot reaching the rope scores 4 runs, or 6 if it clears the rope.

Innings

A team's turn to bat, ending when 10 batters are out or overs finish.

Where It Is Loved

Cricket is followed passionately in Pakistan, India, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka, England, Australia, South Africa, New Zealand and the West Indies. Big events include the ICC World Cup and T20 leagues played every year.

Spirit of cricket: respect for the umpire, the opponent and the rules is a big part of the game.

⚽ Football

The beautiful game: two teams, one ball, and a whole world watching.

About the Game

Football (soccer) is played between two teams of eleven players on a rectangular grass pitch. Players move the ball with their feet, head and body, but only the goalkeeper may use hands inside the penalty area. The team that scores more goals in 90 minutes wins.

Important Rules

  • Offside: an attacker cannot be ahead of the last defender when the ball is passed to them.
  • Fouls: tripping or pushing gives a free kick, or a penalty inside the box.
  • Cards: a yellow card is a warning and a red card sends the player off.
The FIFA World Cup is the biggest tournament, and clubs also play leagues and cups every season.

🏀 Basketball

Fast, high-flying and full of points: shoot, pass and score through the hoop.

About the Game

Basketball is played indoors by two teams of five players on a court with a hoop at each end. Teams score by shooting the ball through the opponent's hoop, which is 10 feet (3.05 m) above the ground. The team with the most points at the end wins.

Basic Rules

Dribbling

Players must bounce the ball while moving. Stopping and then dribbling again is a violation.

Travelling

Taking too many steps without dribbling gives the ball to the other team.

Shot Clock

A team must shoot within a set time, which keeps the game fast.

Fouls

Illegal contact gives the other team free throws or possession.

Did You Know?

Basketball was invented in 1891 by Dr. James Naismith, who first used peach baskets as hoops. Today it is played worldwide, from school courts to big professional leagues and the Olympics.

Teamwork matters: great passing often beats one player trying to do everything.
